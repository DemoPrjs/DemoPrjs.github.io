What is Project Patriot Pipeline?

A Direct Path to Mission-Critical Careers

Project Patriot Pipeline highlights the Department of War's premier talent acquisition and development programs. These programs provide opportunities through direct hiring, comprehensive career guidance, and upskilling, to ensure that the dedication and experience of our Service Members, Military Spouses, and Federal Civilians are channeled into roles where they can make the greatest impact. There is support to help you maximize your potential and ensure a successful transition into your next critical career.
