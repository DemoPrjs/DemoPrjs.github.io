Project Mission Next

Secure top-tier talent for your critical operations. Project Mission Next provides Defense Industrial Base companies and the Department of War with direct access to highly skilled, transitioning Marines, seamlessly matching military expertise to your most demanding workforce needs. Currently, this program is only open to active Marines stationed at Camp Lejeune, NC, with the possibility of expanding at a later date.

Project Patriot Forge

Your country still needs your dedication. If a traditional military path isn't an option, Project Patriot Forge offers an alternative route to serve by providing expedited training and hiring into entry level mission-critical occupations directly within the Department of War. This initiative is open to individuals who received a permanently disqualified determination at a Military Entrance Processing Center.

Civilian Patriot Program

Students Wanted!

Launch your civilian career with the Department of War. This program provides high-school and college students with hands-on experience, competitive training, and the opportunity for employment in mission-essential skilled trade and advanced technical jobs.
